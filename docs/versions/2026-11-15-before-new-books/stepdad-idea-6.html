Anthony 描述了一个典型情形：“Lauren 很难为任何事向我交代。妈妈在时，她会问妈妈是否允许；妈妈不在，她就完全绕过我直接做。我发怒、失去控制，她母亲保护她，然后循环一再重复。没有什么比婚姻分裂、在自己家中被边缘化更难受。”

查看对应英文原文
Anthony recounted a typical scenario. “Lauren has a hard time answering to me for anything. When her mom’s around she will ask her for permission, but if not, she bypasses me altogether and just does it. I would get mad and blow my top, her mother would defend her, and the cycle would repeat itself over and over again. There’s nothing worse than feeling divided in your marriage and marginalized in your own home.”

原文段落 2

中文译文 · AI 翻译

对孩子、成人和大家庭成员而言，继父角色有很多不确定性，但没有人质疑母亲的角色。因此你必须继续做家中的主要家长，尤其负责养护、安慰和惩罚。如果单亲时期你没有认真落实后果，现在就必须承担“坏人”的角色，不要以“家中男主人”为由交给丈夫。在他与孩子建立牢固信任之前——这可能需要多年——他还不能承担这个责任。这必须由你负责。

查看对应英文原文
A stepfather’s role has many uncertainties surrounding it for children, adults, and extended family members. But no one questions Mom’s role. That’s why you must stay the chief parent in your home, especially around matters of nurturance, comfort, and punishment. If you were lax in following through with consequences as a single parent, you must pick up the role of being the “bad guy.” Don’t try to hand that off to your husband as “man of the house.” Until he has developed a strong bond of trust with your children, which can take many years, he can’t take on that responsibility. It must fall to you.
