原文段落 1

中文译文 · AI 翻译

你也许听过共同孩子产生“水泥宝宝效应”、能把重组家庭粘合起来的说法。听来不错吧？希望在于，生一个孩子，给每个人一个共同联系的对象。一个孩子在新同母异父弟弟出生后说：“终于有个人与每个人都有亲缘。”这个理论唯一的问题是，支持它的研究很少。共同孩子出生后，重组家庭会经历各式各样的情绪与关系变化，有些积极，有些并不积极。

查看对应英文原文
You may have heard the theory that an ours baby creates a concrete baby effect that helps “cement” stepfamily relationships. 71 Sounds promising, doesn’t it? The hope is that having a child gives everyone someone with whom they can equally relate. As one child said after his new half-brother was born, “At last, someone who is related to everyone.” The only problem with the theory is that there is little research to support the idea. 72 Stepfamilies experience a wide variety of emotional and relational changes after an ours baby is born. Some are encouraging and some aren’t.

原文段落 2

中文译文 · AI 翻译

如果重组家庭的关系总体分为内部人——有血缘关系的人——和外部人——继亲关系，共同孩子可能带来进一步分裂。那些本来就觉得自己被怠慢的孩子，可能嫉妒新生儿得到的时间和关注，从而怨恨同父异母或同母异父的手足。

查看对应英文原文
If the relationships within the stepfamily home are generally divided between insiders (biologically related persons) and outsiders (steprelationships), a mutual child can bring further division. Biological children who already feel slighted may feel jealous of the time and attention a new child receives, thereby causing resentment toward a half-sibling.

原文段落 3

中文译文 · AI 翻译

显然，我无法告诉你应不应该共同生育。这极其复杂，没有可靠方法预测结果及对每个人的影响。但有一点至关重要：检视你的动机和期待，确认生孩子不是不现实幻想的一部分，也不是企图控制或创造家庭和谐。把这个角色压在婴儿身上是不公平的。花一段时间与妻子一起祷告。依据爱、养育孩子的愿望和主的引领，作出这个重要决定。

查看对应英文原文
It’s obvious that I can’t tell you whether you should or should not have an ours baby. It’s tremendously complicated and there isn’t a solid way to predict the outcome or the impact on everyone involved. However, one thing is vitally important: Examine your motives and your expectations, and be certain that having a child is not part of an unrealistic fantasy or an effort to control and/or create family harmony. It is unfair to burden an infant with that role.
