He even missed Luke’s high school graduation party. Samuel blamed his unavailability on issues with Luke’s mother and stepdad (but from my point of view was hard-pressed to justify his behavior). The bottom line for Luke: he lost contact with his dad, felt rejected, and found himself without his father’s validation at a momentous time in his life—high school graduation.

AI译文

显而易见，失去父亲的关爱影响了 Luke 的情绪；不易察觉的是，这又唤醒了父母离异带来的悲伤。失落会让人想起其他失落，因此 Luke 开始重新处理父母当初如何、为何离婚。“如果妈妈当时没有离开爸爸，爸爸今天还会和我们住在一起，”他告诉我，“如果 Gary 没有介入，爸妈本来还能复合。这是爸爸告诉我的。”这些失落叠加在一起，对 Luke 的内心产生累积影响。他变得抑郁、愤怒。

英文原文
What is obvious is how the loss of his dad’s affections impacted Luke’s mood; what is not so easily noticed is how that loss resurrected his grief over his parents’ divorce. Since loss reminds us of loss, Luke began reprocessing how and why his parents divorced in the first place. “If my mom would not have left my dad back then, my dad would still be living with us today,” Luke told me. “And if Gary had not gotten involved with my mom, my parents could have gotten back together again. That’s what my dad told me.” All of these losses combined, having a cumulative impact on Luke’s heart and soul. He became depressed and angry.

AI译文

表面看来，这是 David 在操纵。但他很可能是通过淡化对继父的感情，保护与生父的关系。坚定的忠诚和怕生父生气的心情，使他在两户家庭中改变表现。〔请记住，这种情况是双向的。你和妻子也要谨慎，不要以为孩子讲述另一户家庭的一切都完全准确。孩子可能为了保全与你的关系而筛选信息。要倾听，也要核实。〕

英文原文
On the surface this appears to be an issue of manipulation by David. But likely David is protecting his relationship with his father by downplaying his affections for his stepdad. Steadfast loyalty and the fear of making his father angry is leading him to change who he is in each household. (Keep in mind that this works both ways. You and your wife should be cautious in assuming that everything you hear from the children about life in the other home is entirely accurate. Realize children may filter information with an agenda of preserving the relationship with you. Listen and verify.)

AI译文

处理你与继子女生父关系的首要目标，是让孩子受益。相互尊重当然可以减轻压力，促进你的继父角色，但主要目的仍是让孩子获得安宁。两个家越和谐，继子女越能自由生活、去爱和成长。研究清楚显示，与生父和继父都保持积极关系的孩子，在情绪、学业、经济和心理方面表现更好。相反，两户家庭持续冲突，几乎能预测孩子各方面的不良结果——包括妻子与前夫、你与他的冲突。情况不必如此。当然，冲突来自双方，你无法控制他的行为，但“若是能行，总要尽力与众人和睦”。

英文原文
The primary goal of managing your relationship with the father of your stepchildren is to bring a blessing to the kids. Sure, a mutually respectful relationship reduces stress and advances your role as stepdad, but the primary purpose is to facilitate peace for the children. The more harmony there is between homes, the more your stepchildren are free to live, love, and grow. Research clearly shows that children who have a positive relationship with both their father and stepfather fare better emotionally, academically, economically, and psychologically. 37 By contrast, ongoing conflict between homes is highly predictive of negative outcomes for children on almost every measure 38 (this includes conflict both between your wife and her ex-husband and between you and him). This doesn’t have to be. Of course, conflict is a two-way street and you can’t control how your ex-husband-in-law behaves, but “as far as it depends on you, live at peace” (Romans 12:18).
