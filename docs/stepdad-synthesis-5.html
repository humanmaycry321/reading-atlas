第6章 · 第6章 母亲的智慧：善意的陷阱

Text/16-Chapter06.xhtml#anchor-24-anchor

AI译文

过度保护的母亲与愤怒的继父聚在一起，却都不愿照镜子，会怎样？Tracy 来信说：“丈夫对我青春期女儿提出建议时，我会很防御。一部分原因是他比我严格，要求事情完全按他的方式做，我则比较放松。另一原因是他的说法。他会说：‘你最好告诉她，如果再不改正，就如何如何……’他是在命令我该告诉她什么。若他温和地说：‘我看得出你因 Rebecca 很挫败。我站在你这边，愿意帮助。我对情况有一些想法’，我就更愿意听。”Tracy 提出了好建议：温和、友善地接近彼此，而不是用拒斥的怒气。但还不止如此。

英文原文
What happens when an overprotective mother and an angry stepfather come together—and neither will look in the mirror? Tracy wrote to us at SuccessfulStepfamilies.com: “I am very defensive when my husband makes suggestions about my teenage daughters. Part of it is that he is much stricter than I; he expects jobs to be done exactly the way he would do them. I am more relaxed about this stuff. The other reason I am defensive is because of how he goes about it. He says to me, ‘You better tell her that if she doesn’t straighten up then blah, blah, blah . . .’ He TELLS me what I better tell her. If he could approach me more gently and say, ‘I can see your frustration with Rebecca. I am on your side and would like to help if I can. I have some thoughts about what’s going on,’ I’d be much more willing to listen.” Tracy makes a great suggestion: Approach one another with gentleness and kindness, not rejecting anger. But there’s more.

AI译文

明智的母亲和继父知道怎样照镜子。他们愿意检视自己的动机、情绪、信念及彼此互动，而不是指责。若 Tracy 谦卑地照镜子，也许会看到，她的内疚与自责容易引发防御；也许童年严厉的家长使她反感强硬意见。这些敏感点会迅速引燃焦虑，让她无法平静回应处境和丈夫。

英文原文
Wise moms and stepdads know how to look in the mirror. They are willing to examine their motives, emotions, and beliefs, and how they interact with each other, not point the finger of blame. If Tracy would take a humble look in the mirror, perhaps she would see that her defensiveness is easily sparked by her guilt and self-blame. Or perhaps she had a harsh parent figure in her childhood and she resents anyone who has strong opinions. These types of sensitivities can quickly ignite her anxiety and steal her ability to calmly respond to the situation and her husband.

第9章 · 第9章 自己的孩子需要什么

Text/19-Chapter09.xhtml#anchor-27-anchor

AI译文

孩子周末来探望时，会拥抱一些人，和另一些人握手；过一会儿，她的孩子回到她身边，我的孩子回到我身边。起初我们以为必须一直把大家聚在一起，他们才会喜欢彼此。但后来明白，让他们去感到安全的地方没有问题。我们让他们保有空间、做自己的事，尊重他们何时、如何选择聚到一起。我们放松、不启动搅拌机后，情况变好了。我们让他们在长时间、温和的火候中慢慢融合。

英文原文
When my kids came to visit for the weekend, they would hug some and shake hands with others, but after a while, her kids would migrate back to her and mine to me. At first we thought we had to group everyone together all the time so they would like each other. But we learned that it was okay to let them go where it was safe. We let them have their own space and do their own thing, and respected when and how they chose to come together. Things got better when we relaxed and didn’t throw on the blender switch. We let them simmer and come together with a long, moderate heat.

AI译文

让孩子在自己的时间里决定对继母和继兄弟姐妹的开放与喜爱程度。同时也为家庭慢炖锅提供温和火候：若所有人自然感兴趣，就鼓励共同活动，并营造有利于关系发展的氛围。例如，要求彼此基本尊重与礼貌。孩子若不相信别人尊重自己的空间与财物，就不会想发展关系。最后，允许他们爱生母、忠诚于她。悖论是，强求他们爱继母，可能反而加强他们对生母的忠诚；若坚持尊重生母，却让爱继母成为自己的选择，他们也许更愿意靠近妻子。〔请明确：尊重、礼貌地对待妻子不是可选项，但爱和亲近感是。〕区别在于自由。强求爱时更难获得爱；爱是自由选择时，可能更早发现它。

英文原文
Let your kids work out their degree of openness to and fondness for their stepmom and stepsiblings in their own time. But balance this with sources of low heat for the family Crockpot: Encourage shared activities when it is of natural interest to everyone involved, and create a climate that fosters relationship development. For example, expect basic respect and courteous behavior toward one another. Until children trust that others respect their personal space and property, they won’t care to deepen their relationships with one another. And finally, be sure to give your children permission to love and remain loyal to their biological mother (see chapter 4). The paradox is this: If you insist they love their stepmom, you may inadvertently embolden their loyalty to their mother, but if you insist on mother respect and make love for their stepmom their choice, they may feel freer to gravitate toward your wife. (To be clear, respectful and courteous behavior toward your wife is not optional, but love and feelings of affection are.) The difference is freedom. When love is demanded, it becomes more difficult to obtain. But when love is a free choice, it can be discovered sooner.

第11章 · 第11章 节日与假期

AI译文

在 Meredith 高中三年级之前，一切改变了。Carly 希望她找暑期工作，全天住在他们家。Andy 说：“妻子决定整个暑假都让女儿住在这里，对我打击极大。经历十个月与继女种种难堪互动后，暑假原本是我疗愈和恢复的时间。在我看来，她完全可以在父亲那里找到暑期工作，但 Carly 想让她留在我们这里。直到她上大学前，那两年非常难熬。”

英文原文
That all changed before Meredith’s junior year in high school. Carly wanted Meredith to get a summer job and live with them full time. Andy shared, “My wife’s decision to have her daughter with us for the WHOLE summer was devastating to me. The summer was my time of healing and recuperation following ten months of dealing with all the ugliness of my stepdaughter. In my humble view, she could easily have gotten a summer job with her dad, but Carly wanted her with us. It was a very brutal two years until she went away to college.”

AI译文

这个故事凸显继父有时感到的无力。Andy 对儿子的事情很有发言权，却几乎不能影响妻子为继女所作的安排。毫无疑问，这令人挫败。我希望能提出简单、非黑即白、解决一切的方法，例如“母亲永远不该单方面决定”，或“继父永远不该怨恨对暑期探望安排没多少选择的孩子”，但这些方法很少经得起生活检验。父母应该遵循的原则，是充分讨论眼前处境，找到对彼此和孩子都宽厚的应对方式。

英文原文
This story highlights the powerlessness that stepdads sometimes feel. Even though Andy had a strong say in what happened with his son, he didn’t have much influence at all in the arrangements made by his wife for his stepdaughter. This is frustrating without a doubt. I wish I could present an easy black-and-white solution that would fix all such circumstances—for example, “Moms should never make unilateral decisions” or “Stepdads should never resent a child who has little choice over their summer visitation schedule”—but such solutions rarely hold up under the scrutiny of life. Rather, the principle for parents to apply is to communicate a lot about the circumstances with which you are presented, and find a way to bring grace to the situation for each other and for the children.
