Luke 大约四岁时，继父 Gary 就进入他的生活。多年来他们相处不错，但最近 Luke 与生父的关系变化改变了这一点。生父 Samuel 原本持续参与他的生活，后来却退出了。Samuel 开始和一个有四个孩子的女人交往，把所有时间花在他们身上，甚至错过 Luke 的高中毕业聚会。他将无暇陪伴归咎于 Luke 母亲和继父的问题，但在我看来，这很难为他的行为辩护。对 Luke 而言，结果是失去与父亲的联系，感到被拒绝，并在高中毕业这样重要的时刻得不到父亲的认可。

查看对应英文原文
Luke’s stepfather, Gary, had been part of his life since he was around four years old. For many years they got along pretty well, but recent transitions in Luke’s relationship with his father had changed that. After many years of consistent involvement, Luke’s dad, Samuel, dropped out of Luke’s life. Samuel started dating a woman with four children and spent all of his time with them. He even missed Luke’s high school graduation party. Samuel blamed his unavailability on issues with Luke’s mother and stepdad (but from my point of view was hard-pressed to justify his behavior). The bottom line for Luke: he lost contact with his dad, felt rejected, and found himself without his father’s validation at a momentous time in his life—high school graduation.

中文译文 · AI 翻译

显而易见，失去父亲的关爱影响了 Luke 的情绪；不易察觉的是，这又唤醒了父母离异带来的悲伤。失落会让人想起其他失落，因此 Luke 开始重新处理父母当初如何、为何离婚。“如果妈妈当时没有离开爸爸，爸爸今天还会和我们住在一起，”他告诉我，“如果 Gary 没有介入，爸妈本来还能复合。这是爸爸告诉我的。”这些失落叠加在一起，对 Luke 的内心产生累积影响。他变得抑郁、愤怒。

查看对应英文原文
What is obvious is how the loss of his dad’s affections impacted Luke’s mood; what is not so easily noticed is how that loss resurrected his grief over his parents’ divorce. Since loss reminds us of loss, Luke began reprocessing how and why his parents divorced in the first place. “If my mom would not have left my dad back then, my dad would still be living with us today,” Luke told me. “And if Gary had not gotten involved with my mom, my parents could have gotten back together again. That’s what my dad told me.” All of these losses combined, having a cumulative impact on Luke’s heart and soul. He became depressed and angry.
