Barrett met Gabrielle through an online dating service. Both divorced empty-nesters with active careers, Barrett had three adult children and Gabrielle two. Gabrielle had a complicated divorce and an ex-husband who often put guilt trips on their children. Accordingly, Gabrielle’s relationship with her kids was strained. When she started dating Barrett, her children pulled back even further. Then, just two weeks before the wedding, her oldest son, age twenty-nine, announced that he wasn’t coming. Gabrielle was crushed and Barrett felt horrible for her.

原文段落 3

中文译文 · AI 翻译

失落及害怕更多失落。在因死亡失去父母一方、或因离婚失去原有家庭之后，任何年龄的孩子都可能害怕失去剩下的东西。一些成年继子女害怕被母亲抛下、隔离。必须与你分享母亲，就意味着失去她的部分时间与精力；若继父还有占用她时间的孩子或孙辈，就更如此。这种恐惧常表现为愤怒，或抗拒与你建立关系。

查看对应英文原文
Loss and the Fear of More Loss. After losing one parent to death, or the family to divorce, children at any age fear losing what remains. Some adult stepchildren fear abandonment or isolation from their parent. When the children must share their mom with you, it means a loss of her time and energy. This is especially true if the stepdad has children or grandchildren who also occupy the mom’s time. This fear is often expressed as anger or resistance in developing a relationship with you.
