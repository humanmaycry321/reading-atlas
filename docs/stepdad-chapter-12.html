原文段落 1

中文译文 · AI 翻译

“乐趣因素”的一个关键是共同休闲。换言之，分开度过的休闲时间不算。Craig 热衷高尔夫，一有机会就打球，也看电视转播，每两年买一套新球杆。妻子 Michelle 喜欢跑步和木雕。两人尊重对方的喜好，也在家中互相补位，让另一方享受爱好。然而，他们很少共同做事。各自的活动带来个人享受并有益身体健康，但缺少共同休闲会对婚姻产生负面影响。

查看对应英文原文
One key aspect of the Fun Factor is that leisure time be spent together. In other words, leisure time spent apart doesn’t count. Craig is an avid golfer. He plays a round of golf every chance possible. He also watches golf on TV and purchases a new set of clubs every two years. His wife, Michelle, enjoys running and wood carving. Each spouse respects the other’s preference and “covers the bases” at home so the other can enjoy their hobby. However, Craig and Michelle rarely do anything together. Their separate activities provide personal enjoyment and aid physical health, but a lack of shared leisure activity will negatively impact their marriage.

原文段落 2

中文译文 · AI 翻译

关系开始时，打高尔夫的 Craig 与跑步的 Michelle 确实一起享受过晚餐、电影等快乐时光。但家庭、工作、教会、孩子运动活动及其他责任带来的压力与忙乱，挤走了这一关键因素。如果希望婚姻健康，明智的做法是恢复、找回已失去的东西。

查看对应英文原文
At the beginning of their relationship, Craig (the golfer) and Michelle (the runner) did share fun times together such as dinner and a movie. But the pressures and the frenzy of family, work, church, kids’ sporting events, and other responsibilities have squeezed out this crucial element. If they desire a healthy marriage, they would be wise to restore and recover what has been lost.
