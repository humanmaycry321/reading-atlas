原文段落 1

中文译文 · AI 翻译

过度保护的母亲与愤怒的继父聚在一起，却都不愿照镜子，会怎样？Tracy 来信说：“丈夫对我青春期女儿提出建议时，我会很防御。一部分原因是他比我严格，要求事情完全按他的方式做，我则比较放松。另一原因是他的说法。他会说：‘你最好告诉她，如果再不改正，就如何如何……’他是在命令我该告诉她什么。若他温和地说：‘我看得出你因 Rebecca 很挫败。我站在你这边，愿意帮助。我对情况有一些想法’，我就更愿意听。”Tracy 提出了好建议：温和、友善地接近彼此，而不是用拒斥的怒气。但还不止如此。

查看对应英文原文
What happens when an overprotective mother and an angry stepfather come together—and neither will look in the mirror? Tracy wrote to us at SuccessfulStepfamilies.com: “I am very defensive when my husband makes suggestions about my teenage daughters. Part of it is that he is much stricter than I; he expects jobs to be done exactly the way he would do them. I am more relaxed about this stuff. The other reason I am defensive is because of how he goes about it. He says to me, ‘You better tell her that if she doesn’t straighten up then blah, blah, blah . . .’ He TELLS me what I better tell her. If he could approach me more gently and say, ‘I can see your frustration with Rebecca. I am on your side and would like to help if I can. I have some thoughts about what’s going on,’ I’d be much more willing to listen.” Tracy makes a great suggestion: Approach one another with gentleness and kindness, not rejecting anger. But there’s more.

原文段落 2

中文译文 · AI 翻译

明智的母亲和继父知道怎样照镜子。他们愿意检视自己的动机、情绪、信念及彼此互动，而不是指责。若 Tracy 谦卑地照镜子，也许会看到，她的内疚与自责容易引发防御；也许童年严厉的家长使她反感强硬意见。这些敏感点会迅速引燃焦虑，让她无法平静回应处境和丈夫。

查看对应英文原文
Wise moms and stepdads know how to look in the mirror. They are willing to examine their motives, emotions, and beliefs, and how they interact with each other, not point the finger of blame. If Tracy would take a humble look in the mirror, perhaps she would see that her defensiveness is easily sparked by her guilt and self-blame. Or perhaps she had a harsh parent figure in her childhood and she resents anyone who has strong opinions. These types of sensitivities can quickly ignite her anxiety and steal her ability to calmly respond to the situation and her husband.
